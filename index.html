A little
about my
practice
& I

If I had to blame something, it'd probably be growing up between many countries (don't get me wrong, I wouldn't have it any other way). Learning to balance these cultural worlds has made somewhere-in-between feel pretty normal to me. So naturally, I gravitate towards plural perspectives and work that makes you feel like something was made with you in mind.

Hi, I'm Nupur, a creative thinker, maker, and designer

I am attuned to an endless, diverse spectrum of culture. I pull from films I love, art that inspires me, everyday observations that live as scribbles in my journals. All of that shapes how I build context before form.

I'm interested in the space between strategy, art, creative direction, and experiences, where an idea can become more than a single designed artefact.
