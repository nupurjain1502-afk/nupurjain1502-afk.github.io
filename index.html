about my
practice
& I

I approach creative work through curiosity about people: what they notice, avoid, linger on, and connect with. I want to give people a reason to recognise themselves, partake, or remember something.

At its best, design makes you feel that something was made with you in mind.

about me

Hi, I'm Nupur, a creative thinker, maker, and designer

If I had to 'blame' something…

it'd probably be growing up across different cultural worlds (don't get me wrong, I wouldn't have it any other way). Though, the numerous countries I've lived in have made somewhere-in-between feel pretty normal to me, so naturally I gravitate towards projects and practices that hold plural perspectives and use design as a form of recognition.

I pull from films I love, art that inspires me, everyday observations that live as scribbles in my journals. All of that shapes how I build context before form.

I'm building a practice that sits between strategy and experience: conceptual and immersive, designing for inclusion, for social causes, and for the kind of resonance that stays with people.

A collection of exploratory works and passion projects shaping the foundations of my creative process and tracing my interest in materiality.

Portraiture Great Grandmother's Gaze 2022 · charcoal / observational drawing / expressive mark-making

A series exploring memory, emotion and identity through portraiture and tactile image-making. Working primarily with charcoal, these pieces focus on atmosphere, intimacy and emotional presence foundations that continue to shape my design practice today.

Selected Photography 2026 · Photography / Visual Storytelling

A curated selection of photographs taken and edited over several years. The series reflects my instinct for observation, composition and visual storytelling. The work explores the details, people and places that capture my attention, revealing how photography continues to inform my research, creative direction and broader design practice.
