about me

Hi, I'm Nupur narrative-led designer, deep thinker & storyteller.

My practice is shaped by an arts background and a growing interest in strategy and experience. I'm drawn to work that explores cultural identity, belonging and perspective, often inspired by people, places, and their stories.

I tend to approach projects through research and narrative, pulling from film, art, and everyday observations to build context before form. I'm drawn to culturally attuned work: projects that hold plural perspectives, sit between traditions, and use design as a form of recognition rather than decoration.

I am interested in building a practice that sits between strategy and experience, where design is both conceptual and immersive designing for inclusion, for social causes, and for the kind of emotional resonance that stays with people long after they've moved on.

Brand Strategy & Identity Concept

Held Within

2025 brand strategy / identity / storytelling

A concept-led brand system exploring storytelling for third-culture identities. Responding to the brief of creating an "origin brand," I developed Held Within as a hybrid print-digital platform centred on belonging, memory, and in-between cultural experiences. Driven by research into diasporic identity, the brand honours plural identities through a cohesive visual language across print, digital, and spatial applications. I approached the identity as a narrative system, considering how my targeted audience would move through, emotionally engage with, and see themselves reflected within the brand.

Installation Art

A Place at Every Table

2022 installation / relational / multisensory

A relational installation exploring immigration, assimilation, and cultural memory through food, fabric, conversation, and participation. Centred around my family dining table, the work mapped the countries, cuisines, recipes, and stories that shaped my upbringing across multiple cultural contexts. Audience participation was central viewers were invited to eat directly from the installation, allowing spills, stains, and traces of interaction to physically alter the artwork over time.

Speculative Service Design / UX Strategy / Civic Systems Design

Mars Integration Network

2026 speculative design / UX strategy / civic systems

This speculative service design project reimagines permanent residency assessment through two parallel systems of value: Earth-Admin, which measures applicants through economic productivity and institutional classification, and Mars-Local, which evaluates contribution through collective survival, adaptability, and community resilience.

Using service journeys, civic artefacts, environmental storyboards, and UI systems, I explored how institutions translate human experience into administrative value. By relocating the same procedure into a resource-dependent Martian settlement, the project questions how different environments produce different definitions of worth, belonging, and citizenship.

Project part of RMIT University — Experimental Experience Design Studio, a 12-week speculative design course exploring alternative models of permanent residency assessment (Mars-Local).

Service Design · Research · Service Blueprint · Prototyping

State Library Companion

2026 service design / research / service blueprint / prototyping

Developed for State Library Victoria, this fictional service design project explores how personalised guidance can improve the discoverability of spaces, resources and services within complex public environments.

It explores how service design can strengthen the role of public libraries as inclusive civic and cultural spaces. The resulting concept acts as a connective layer between people, places and information, supporting more accessible and meaningful engagement with public knowledge spaces.
